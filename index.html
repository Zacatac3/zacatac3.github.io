Clashboard

Track Clash of Clans upgrades with beautiful iOS Home Screen widgets.

iOS-only • Widgets for Home Screen

Download (App Store)

What it does

  • Shows current upgrade progress for troops, heroes, and buildings.
  • Multiple widget sizes: small, medium, large.
  • Quick glance timers and percent complete.
  • Lightweight, privacy-first — data stored on device.

How to use

  1. Install Clashboard from the App Store.
  2. Open the app and add your village/upgrades.
  3. Long-press the Home Screen → + → add Clashboard widget.
