How it works


Connect once
Start Python and the HTTPS tunnel on your Mac. Save the tunnel URL and admin token here, then enable iPhone notifications from the installed Home Screen app. A restarted quick tunnel gets a new URL; update it in Connection settings. Your URL and token are saved on this device. Forgetting them does not stop monitoring or unsubscribe pushes.


Monitoring & notifications
Tap Start monitoring after restarting the backend. Keep your Mac awake and connected to the internet. You can close the iPhone app. Checks cover Star White and Night Sky 256GB at Franklin Park and Briarwood.
Every in-stock check sends a separate push. Availability changes also trigger alerts, with a status notification every four hours. Summaries show White / Blue, IN STOCK / OOS, and Eastern Time. Real Duo alerts start with 🚨 DUO IN STOCK.


Polling & cooldowns
Choose 30–300 seconds in 15-second steps; the interval is saved on the Mac. Increasing it can extend the pending wait. Decreasing it applies after the pending check. A failed check raises intervals below 60 seconds to 60. Apple blocks pause requests for 20, then 30, then 60 minutes. Controls and restarts do not bypass cooldowns.


Activity & delivery
The log refreshes every 15 seconds while visible and records checks even with the app closed. The latest 1,440 requests stay in memory until Python restarts. Times use Eastern Time with daylight saving adjustments. Long gaps can indicate sleep or downtime. Push acceptance confirms submission to the push service, not delivery to your phone.


Test mode
Testing replaces Duo with iPhone 18 Pro Burgundy 256GB (MJQ54LL/A) at both stores. A mode change sends fresh status on the next permitted scheduled check and respects cooldowns. Switch it off to restore Duo. Python restarts default to Duo mode.
