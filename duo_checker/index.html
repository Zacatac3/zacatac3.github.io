Duo Stock Monitor

Checks stock every minute and sends a notification on every check while anything is in stock. Availability changes trigger alerts too, plus a status check every 4 hours when everything stays out of stock.

Push summaries use White / Blue and IN STOCK / OOS, with one line per store and an Eastern timestamp to the second. Real Duo stock alerts start with 🚨 DUO IN STOCK. Each in-stock check creates a separate notification; the log keeps every request.

Times use Eastern Time (EDT/EST), to the second, and adjust automatically for daylight saving time. Keep your Mac awake with the backend and HTTPS tunnel running. Open this app from your iPhone Home Screen.

You can close this iPhone app; monitoring and push delivery continue independently on your Mac. Monitored variants: Star White 256GB and Night Sky 256GB, at Franklin Park and Briarwood.

Connection settings will be saved on this device when you save or use them.
