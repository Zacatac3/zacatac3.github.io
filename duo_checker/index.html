Duo Stock Monitor

Checks stock every minute. Get an alert when availability changes, plus a status notification every 4 hours, in stock or out of stock.

Notification times use EST (UTC−5), to the second. Keep your Mac awake with the backend and HTTPS tunnel running. Open this app from your iPhone Home Screen.
